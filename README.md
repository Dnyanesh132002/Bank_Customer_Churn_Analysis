# Bank Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for banks because losing customers directly affects revenue and growth. In this project, I analyzed a bank customer dataset to identify the factors that influence customer churn and understand the characteristics of customers who are more likely to leave the bank.

The analysis was performed using Excel, Python, and SQL. The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), customer segmentation, and SQL-based business analysis to generate meaningful insights.

## Key Insights

* Overall customer churn rate was 20.37%.
* Germany recorded the highest customer churn.
* Female customers showed a higher churn tendency than male customers.
* Older customers were more likely to leave the bank.
* Inactive customers had a significantly higher churn rate.
* Customers with higher account balances were more likely to churn.
* Age showed the strongest positive relationship with churn.
* Active membership reduced the likelihood of customer churn.
